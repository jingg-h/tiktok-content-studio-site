Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

TikTok Content Studio Local is a development and testing project for creating, reviewing, and publishing short-form videos to TikTok.

Information Used by the App

The app may use TikTok authorization data to connect a user's TikTok account for video publishing. This may include access tokens, refresh tokens, account identifiers, and authorized scopes returned by TikTok.

The app may also store local project information such as prompts, generated video status, captions, hashtags, and local file paths.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to support user-initiated video generation, review, and publishing workflows.

Data Sharing

This app does not sell personal data. It does not share personal data with third parties except as needed to use TikTok APIs for actions initiated by the user.

Data Storage

During development, app data may be stored locally on the user's machine.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the app owner.
